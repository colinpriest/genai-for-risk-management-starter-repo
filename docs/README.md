# `docs/` — what goes here

Two things live in this folder, and **both are validity gates**. Neither earns you marks by being
present; each costs you **−6** by being absent.

| Path | What it is | If missing |
|---|---|---|
| `docs/cycle-transcript.md` | Your Cycle session with ChatGPT — an export, or a share link | **−6** |
| `docs/meetings/` | Every team meeting's transcript **and** its minutes | Condition of assessment: your individual contribution mark loses one of its three sources and cannot be awarded above 4 of 6 |

## `docs/cycle-transcript.md`

The whole session, not a summary. The Cycle stage is marked on whether you **challenged** the
model's mechanisms rather than accepting its first answer, and the transcript is the only evidence
of that. A reconstructed-after-the-fact transcript is visible as one and scores zero.

Export from ChatGPT, or paste a share link — either is accepted.

## `docs/meetings/`

One file per meeting, each containing the transcript **and** the minutes. The transcript shows what
was said; the minutes show what the team **decided**, who owns each action, and what is still open.

The minutes are the part that is read. They are also one of the three sources your **individual**
contribution mark comes from — alongside the repository history and both peer-assessment rounds —
so a meeting record that never names who argued for what cannot help you.

See `team-templates/meeting-records-requirements.md` on Moodle for the template, the worked
example, and how recordings are handled.
